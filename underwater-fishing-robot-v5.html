Underwater Fishing Robot

Detect • Catch • Return

Find the target fish, lock the sonar, fire the net, then bring the fish back to the surface.

  • Sonar: keep the target in the cone and hold still until the lock bar fills.
  • Net: fire when the lock is full and the fish is close. Fish dodge, and sharks tear nets.
  • Surface: the net holds only while you move gently. Sharks bite, jellyfish sting, plastic fouls the motors. Buy upgrades after each mission to survive deeper dives.

W/S forward and back · A/D turn · E/Q up and down · P sonar · Space or click to fire

Deep-sea creatures in the abyss pay well but bite back.
